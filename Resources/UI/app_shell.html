Multi-agent crew runs

          

            A crew is a small team of AI agents that work on a goal together:
            
    
              
  • a Supervisor dispatches checkpoints,
    • 
              
  • a Worker builds,
    • 
              
  • a Verifier inspects against success criteria.
    • 
            

            Each role runs in its own chat. Stop / resume any time; checkpoints
            persist across editor restarts.
